The Monroe Street Tattler

Neighborhood news so small, it barely counts.

The Monroe Street Tattler is an occasional publication devoted to matters of local consequence and limited consequence. It began with a french fry report that was, by all accounts, too controversial for conventional neighborhood media. It has continued, since, for reasons that remain under internal review.

New issues appear when neighborhood conditions warrant. There is no schedule. There is only vigilance.

Contribute to the Tattler

The Monroe Street Tattler welcomes food opinions, harmless neighborhood observations, corrections, guest dispatches, comics, cartoons, puzzles, and questions requiring lighthearted investigation.

Know someone who would enjoy writing for a tiny neighborhood paper? Have a student in the house with strong opinions, a drawing, or a puzzle to submit? Have a neighborhood pro tip, an oddly specific local observation, or an overheard gem that deserves preservation?

Write to: MonroeStreetTattler@gmail.com

Submissions may be edited for length, clarity, and unnecessary restraint.
