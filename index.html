About the Tattler

The Monroe Street Tattler is an occasional, unofficial neighborhood paper about local food, neighborhood oddities, and the small details that make a place feel like itself.

The aim is simple: celebrate local places, notice small things, and help build the kind of neighborhood spirit that comes from shared curiosity, good humor, and paying attention to what's right in front of us.

Send a Tip or Write for Us

The Tattler is always accepting leads, questions, tips, curiosities, and small matters that may deserve more attention than they strictly require.

Know someone in the neighborhood worth interviewing? Have a local pro tip others should know? Notice a tiny mystery, excellent sandwich, suspiciously beloved menu item, useful shortcut, unusual sign, strong opinion, or oddly perfect overheard remark?

Send it our way.

Ideas, suggestions, reader submissions, and things that made you say “someone should look into this” can be sent to: MonroeStreetTattler@gmail.com

Send whatever seems worth sharing. The Tattler will take it from there.
