About the Tattler

The Monroe Street Tattler is an occasional, unofficial neighborhood paper about local food, neighborhood oddities, and the small details that make a place feel like itself.

The aim is simple: celebrate local places, notice small things, and help build the kind of neighborhood spirit that comes from shared curiosity, good humor, and paying attention to what's right in front of us.

Contribute to the Tattler

The Tattler welcomes food opinions, harmless neighborhood observations, corrections, guest dispatches, comics, cartoons, puzzles, and questions requiring lighthearted investigation.

Know someone who would enjoy writing for a tiny neighborhood paper? Have a student in the house with strong opinions, a drawing, or a puzzle to submit? Have a neighborhood pro tip, an oddly specific local observation, or an overheard gem that deserves preservation?

Write to: MonroeStreetTattler@gmail.com

Submissions may be edited for length, clarity, and unnecessary restraint.
