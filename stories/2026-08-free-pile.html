Terrace Free Pile Raises Philosophical Questions

Dispatch

Another new box of assorted household items appeared near the sidewalk this week, prompting residents to consider the thin line between upcycling and making garbage available to the public.

By afternoon, several items had found new homes. The remaining object, described by one passerby as “possibly a lid,” awaits further review.
