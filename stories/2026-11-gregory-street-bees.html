Bees Assert Control Over Gregory Street

A reader spotted a homemade sign outside a house near Gregory and Baltzell, all bearing the same warning: CAUTION BEES.

As the reader observed, “In most other neighborhoods this would be a job for an exterminator, but in ours, we adapt to their schedule.”
