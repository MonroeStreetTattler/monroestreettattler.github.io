Ready to Report?

Use the official submission form. It asks for your panel name, how many tasters, and your family awards, and it takes about two minutes.

The form asks whether we may publish your findings and whether you would like to be named or kept anonymous. Results may appear in a future issue. You can also just email MonroeStreetTattler@gmail.com if you would rather.
