    Where are the Santas?

    The map covers Monroe Street and nearby blocks in Madison, including parts of Nakoma and Odana. Pins show neighbor-reported outdoor displays.

    Which Santas count?

    The main count includes only the matching 12-foot Santa shown above, holding a candy cane and green present. Other inflatable Santas have a separate count and appear when you turn on “Show all Santas.”

    How do I add one?

    Choose the matching Santa or other Santa button, enter the display’s street address, and confirm its location before submitting.

    Are all the Santas out right now?

    Sightings are reported by neighbors. Displays may change, and an inflatable may be deflated or switched off when you visit. View displays from public streets and sidewalks, and respect residents’ property.
