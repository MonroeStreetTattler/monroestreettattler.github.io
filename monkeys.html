Official Notice

The Great Monkey
Escape of 2026

Presented by The Monroe Street Tattler + Orange Tree Imports

38 monkeys are loose again on Monroe Street.

Once again, a number of monkeys have escaped, but this time are currently hiding in business windows throughout the neighborhood.

Find any 10 of the 38 monkeys, write down each one's name and the business where you spotted it, and return your completed tracking form to Orange Tree Imports by October 31 to enter the Great Monkey Adoption Drawing.

Download the Official Tracking Form
Printable · One page · Print as many as you need

Already have the October Tattler? The tracking form is printed inside. Cut it out and you are ready to go — no need to download anything.

Don’t have a copy? Download and print the form above. Entries are turned in on paper, so you will need a printed form to enter the drawing.

No printer? Pick up a blank tracking form at Orange Tree Imports. They are also where completed forms go, so it is one stop either way.

How it works

  1. 38 monkeys are hidden in neighborhood business windows.
  2. Find any 10 of them.
  3. Record each monkey's name and the business where you found it.
  4. Complete your name, email, and phone number on the tracking form.
  5. Return the completed form to Orange Tree Imports by October 31.
  • All ages are welcome.
  • One entry per participant.
  • Multiple members of the same household may each submit their own entry.
  • This is a random drawing, not first come, first served.
  • 38 winners will be drawn.
  • All 38 monkeys will ultimately be adopted.

Why “again”?

In 1960, monkeys famously escaped from Vilas Zoo. Sixty-six years later, the problem has apparently returned.

New here?

The Monroe Street Tattler is a neighborhood newspaper devoted to hyperlocal news. If you found this page by scanning a monkey in a window, this is what you have wandered into. The October issue carries the hunt and the tracking form.
